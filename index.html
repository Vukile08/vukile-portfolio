About the Artist

Mzuvukile Norman Nxuseka is a South African visual artist whose practice spans painting, sculpture, and digital art. His works explore identity, memory, and transformation, bridging traditional forms with contemporary expressions. Nxuseka’s career highlights include:

  • Inclusion in the Smithsonian Libraries Artist Files
  • Exhibitions and projects with Artist Proof Studio (2018–2023), including The Trinity Session – Profitroom and ABSA Body–Place–Space Alumni 2021
  • Works featured in auctions and online platforms: MutualArt, ArtPrice, Strauss & Co, Lot-Art, and TAF
  • Press coverage: IOL / Cape Times – Student Riot 1976
